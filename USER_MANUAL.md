## For Guests

### Welcome to the Wedding Website
The homepage welcomes you with the couple's names, a live countdown to the wedding day, and the venue details. Scroll down to explore the full story.

### Navigation
The top navbar contains:
- **6 primary links**: Our Story, The Day, RSVP, Songbook, Guests, FAQ
- **PLAN button**: Opens the wedding planner (couple only, requires password)
- **BEFORE | AFTER toggle**: Switch between the anticipation mode (BEFORE the wedding) and the memory mode (AFTER the wedding)
- **More menu** (⋮ icon): Contains secondary links (Home, Venue, Travel), QR & Share, Theme toggle (Light/Dark/System), Language toggle (EN/SN), and Logout

### RSVP
1. Click "RSVP" in the navbar or scroll to the RSVP section
2. Fill in your full name, email, and attendance choice
3. Select your meal preference (Beef, Chicken, Vegetarian, Vegan, Traditional Zimbabwean)
4. Note any dietary requirements
5. Indicate if you're bringing a plus-one or children
6. Request a song for the dance floor
7. Leave a personal message for the couple
8. Click "Send Your RSVP" — you'll see a gold sparkle animation on success

### Songbook
The songbook contains 26 songs across 4 tabs:
- **Ceremony**: Processional, bridal entrance, recessional
- **Reception**: 19 songs including Zimbabwean artists (Oliver Mtukudzi, Alick Macheso, Mokoomba)
- **First Dance**: The couple's first dance selections
- **Guest Requests**: Submit your own song request + vote on others

Click the heart icon on any song to vote for it. The DJ will see the live-ranked list on the wedding day.

### QR Check-in
On the wedding day, scan the QR code at the venue entrance to check in. This unlocks:
- The live photo wall
- Real-time song requests
- Applause and message features
- Your personal greeting from the couple

### Meet Our Village (Guest Contributions)
The "Meet Our Village" section displays memories, blessings, advice, and wishes from invited guests. Filter by type using the chips at the top (All, Memories, Advice, Blessings, Funny Stories, Wishes).

### Photo Gallery
View engagement photos and pre-wedding moments in the gallery. Filter by moment type (Ceremony, Reception, Candid, etc.). Click any photo to open the full-screen lightbox.

### Share the Wedding
Use the "Spread the Love" section to share the wedding website via WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, Email, or copy the link. A QR code is also available for download and printing.

### Language Toggle
Switch between English (EN) and Shona (SN) using the language toggle in the "More" dropdown. Navigation labels translate instantly.

### Theme Toggle
Switch between Light, Dark, and System themes using the theme toggle in the "More" dropdown. Your preference persists across sessions.

## For the Couple

### Logging In
1. Click the **"Couple Login"** button at the bottom-left of the screen
2. Enter the password: `wewed-admin-2026`
3. Edit mode turns ON automatically
4. A gold banner appears: "✏️ Edit Mode is ON — Click any gold pencil icon to edit text"
5. 26 gold pencil icons appear next to editable text throughout the site

### Editing Content
1. Click any **gold pencil icon** next to text you want to edit
2. A dialog opens with a textarea pre-filled with the current text
3. Edit the text and click "Save" — changes appear instantly and persist in your browser
4. Click "Reset to original" to revert any change

**Editable sections include:**
- Hero: Bride's name, Groom's name, Wedding date, Venue, Tagline
- Our Story: Section heading, subtitle, 5 milestone titles + stories, family portrait
- The Day: Section heading, date/venue line, venue name, venue description, dress code

### Turning Off Edit Mode
- Click the **"Editing"** button (bottom-left) to toggle edit mode OFF
- Click the **logout icon** to log out completely

### Before/After Toggle
The BEFORE | AFTER toggle in the navbar switches the entire site between:
- **BEFORE**: Anticipation mode — story, venue, programme, RSVP, travel, songbook, guests, vendors, check-in, gallery, capsule, live wall, village, FAQ, pricing, vision, merch
- **AFTER**: Memory mode — recap film, photo gallery, media upload, live wall, village, capsule, vendors, registry, FAQ, pricing, vision, merch

## For Contributors

### Your Personal Contribution Link
Every invited guest receives a unique, secure link that looks like:
```
https://wewed.app/?contribute=YOUR_TOKEN
```

### Writing Your Message
1. Open your contribution link
2. You'll see a focused editor — "Leave Your Message for Charity & Kudzie"
3. Fill in:
   - **Display Name** (pre-filled from guest list)
   - **Relationship to couple** (e.g. "Charity's university friend")
   - **Contribution Type**: Memory, Advice, Blessing, Funny Story, or Wish
   - **Your Message** (max 500 words / 2,500 characters — a live counter shows your count)
   - **Favorite Song** (optional)
   - **Privacy**: Public, Couple Only, or Anonymous
4. Click "Save Draft" to save without submitting, or "Submit for Review" to send to the couple

### After Submitting
- Your contribution enters "Pending Review" status
- The couple receives a notification in their admin dashboard
- They can: Approve, Reject, Request Changes, Feature, or Hide
- Once approved, your contribution appears in the "Meet Our Village" gallery on the website

### Rules
- Maximum 500 words / 2,500 characters
- No HTML, JavaScript, or links
- No phone numbers, email addresses, or addresses
- No profanity or inappropriate language
- Maximum 10 edits before submission
- After approval, further edits need re-approval

## The Admin Dashboard

### Accessing the Dashboard
Press **Ctrl+Shift+A** (or Cmd+Shift+A on Mac) on any page.
Enter password: `wewed-admin-2026`

### 6 Tabs

1. **Overview**: Stats cards (days until wedding, RSVPs, acceptance rate, checked-in guests), recent activity feed
2. **RSVPs**: Full RSVP management — search, filter, CSV export, manual check-in toggle, expandable rows with full details
3. **Songbook**: View all songs with vote counts, mark as "played", reorder, add new songs, see guest requests
4. **Messages**: Guest wall messages — approve, hide, remove; time capsule message count
5. **Ceremony**: Live ceremony control — start ceremony, advance to next item, trigger moments (cake cutting, first dance, last dance), live check-in count
6. **Contributions**: Guest contribution moderation queue — approve, reject, feature, hide submissions; generate tokens for all guests; copy token links to share

### Generating Contribution Tokens
1. Open Admin Dashboard (Ctrl+Shift+A)
2. Go to the **Contributions** tab
3. Click **"Generate Tokens"**
4. All guests receive unique secure tokens
5. Copy the token links and share with guests via WhatsApp/email
6. Sample bridal party contributions are auto-created for demo

## Collaborative Guest Contributions

### How It Works
1. **Couple** generates tokens in the admin dashboard
2. **Guest** opens their unique link (`?contribute=TOKEN`)
3. **Guest** writes their message (memory, advice, blessing, funny story, or wish)
4. **Guest** submits for review
5. **Couple** reviews in the admin dashboard → Approve/Reject/Feature
6. **Public** sees approved contributions in the "Meet Our Village" gallery

### Anti-Abuse System
- 500 word / 2,500 character limit (server-enforced)
- Profanity filter (36-word blocklist)
- No HTML, JavaScript, or links allowed
- No phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses
- Rate limit: maximum 10 edits before submission
- Revision history: every edit saved, admin can compare and restore

### Privacy Options
- **Public**: Visible to all site visitors
- **Couple Only**: Visible only to the couple in the admin dashboard
- **Anonymous**: Display name replaced with "Anonymous"

## FAQ

### How do I RSVP?
Click "RSVP" in the navbar, fill in the form, and click "Send Your RSVP". You'll see a gold sparkle confirmation.

### How do I vote for songs?
Go to the Songbook section, click the heart icon next to any song. Your vote is saved instantly.

### How do I switch between BEFORE and AFTER mode?
Click the "BEFORE" or "AFTER" button in the navbar. The entire site changes between anticipation mode and memory mode.

### How do I change the theme?
Click the "More" (⋮) button in the navbar, then use the theme toggle (sun/moon icon) to switch between Light, Dark, and System modes.

### How do I change the language?
Click the "More" (⋮) button, then use the EN/SN toggle to switch between English and Shona.

### How do I share the wedding website?
Scroll to the "Spread the Love" section. Use the share buttons for WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, Email, or copy the link. A QR code is also available for download.

### How do I contribute a message?
You need a personal contribution link from the couple. Open the link, write your message, and submit. The couple will review and approve it.

### How do I access the wedding planner?
Click the "PLAN" button in the navbar. Enter the password: `wewed-admin-2026`. The planner is for the couple and their team only.

### How do I edit the website content?
Click "Couple Login" at the bottom-left. Enter the password. Edit mode turns ON automatically — look for gold pencil icons next to editable text. Click any pencil to edit.

### What is the AI assistant?
The floating gold button (bottom-right) opens the wewed AI assistant, powered by GLM 5.2. It can answer questions about the wedding (timing, dress code, venue, dietary options, Zimbabwean traditions). Inside the planner, there's also an AI tab for vow generation, speech writing, and budget advice.

## Technical Information

- **Framework**: Next.js 16 with App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, shadcn/ui
- **Database**: Prisma + SQLite (22 models)
- **Real-time**: Socket.io mini-service on port 3003
- **AI**: GLM 5.2 via z-ai-web-dev-sdk
- **PWA**: Installable, offline-capable service worker
- **APIs**: 34 routes covering RSVP, songs, media, messages, QR codes, privacy, planner, AI, Telegram, content editing, import/export, and guest contributions
