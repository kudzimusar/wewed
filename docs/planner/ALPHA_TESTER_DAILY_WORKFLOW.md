# Alpha tester daily planner workflow

This workflow is the operational baseline for testing the selected wedding from early planning through event execution and closeout.

## 1. Plan

Open the **Planning workspace**, confirm the active wedding, and review Tasks, Budget, Vendors, Guests, Timeline, and Seating. Record decisions in the module where they will be used rather than in disconnected notes.

## 2. Coordinate

Use Planner tools to coordinate the assigned team, vendors, approvals, documents, invitations, and client profile. Confirm that every action remains scoped to the selected wedding.

## 3. Update

Update changing facts as they become authoritative: task owners and dates, vendor operational status, payments, guest RSVP information, Timeline times, and Seating assignments. Use worksheet recovery for controlled bulk changes and retain rollback evidence.

## 4. Operate

Before the wedding day, verify the complete run sheet and live Seating plan. Confirm available and full tables, unassigned Guests, accessibility and dietary notes, vendor arrival details, and the responsibilities of the day-of team.

## 5. Execute

On the wedding day, work from the active Timeline and Seating views. Move Guests or parties between tables only when capacity allows, update table or zone details, record arrivals and changes, and keep the printable operational documentation current.

## 6. Close

After the event, complete outstanding tasks, record final vendor and Budget outcomes, preserve import and audit history, export the final documentation, and verify that no data from another wedding was changed.
