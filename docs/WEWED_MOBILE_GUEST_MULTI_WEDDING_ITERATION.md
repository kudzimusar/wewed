# Wewed Mobile Guest Multi-Wedding Iteration

Status: implementation/qualification on PR #202. Do not merge until the exact head passes repository CI, invitation browser UAT and Android qualification.

## Product contract

The approved Ivory Floral Gold invitation remains frozen. This iteration changes guest persistence, mobile wedding identity and sharing behavior around it.

### 1. Multi-wedding guest portfolio

A guest may have legitimate invitation relationships with multiple weddings. Wewed retains a bounded, signed, HttpOnly portfolio of wedding-scoped guest identities and a last-active wedding pointer.

The portfolio stores wedding/guest identity references and invitation presentation style. It does **not** store RSVP tokens. Selecting a wedding must revalidate the signed wedding/guest pair against the database and obtain the active RSVP credential server-side.

Wedding A credentials must never authorize Wedding B.

### 2. Cold launch contract

Cold app launch resolves in this order:

1. pending explicit invitation handoff;
2. authenticated Admin/Couple/Planner/Vendor workspace;
3. last-active invited-guest wedding;
4. sign-in fallback.

A guest-only cold launch opens the last-active wedding's invitation card first. Authenticated management roles are never hijacked by a remembered guest wedding. Warm Android resume remains the responsibility of the Android/TWA activity stack and should preserve the current screen.

### 3. Wedding switcher

For invited guests with more than one retained wedding, the mobile wedding identity control exposes **My Weddings**. Selecting another wedding:

1. revalidates that guest/wedding relationship;
2. makes it the active wedding;
3. creates the scoped active guest session;
4. opens that wedding's invitation card, not its Couple Site.

`Leave Wedding` removes only the current wedding. If another wedding remains, `/app` restores the next active invitation.

### 4. Mobile identity surface

The mobile top identity surface uses a champagne/gold treatment with espresso text/icons, while the bottom navigation remains espresso. The current couple names/date and wedding monogram remain recognizable and safe-area aware.

The existing bottom navigation remains:

`Home · RSVP · My Wedding · More`

The existing right-side More drawer remains unchanged in structure.

### 5. Private-wedding sharing

For a private/invitation-only wedding:

- the **wedding website URL** may be shared;
- sharing the website does not invite another person and does not grant RSVP access;
- the Share control must never copy the current invitation URL;
- personal invitation URLs, RSVP tokens, guest identity, install-handoff credentials and guest QR codes must never be included in the share payload;
- no generic private-wedding QR is generated by the Couple Site share section;
- each invited guest keeps their own invitation relationship and unique QR.

The governing distinction is:

> The website identifies the wedding. The invitation identifies the guest.

### 6. Entry-path preservation

Personal invitation paths add the successfully resolved wedding to the guest portfolio.

Physical invitation paths remain anonymous/shared until a guest successfully claims the invitation. Only then is the identified guest/wedding relationship added to the portfolio.

Deferred Android personal invitation recovery adds the recovered wedding to the same portfolio without exposing the RSVP credential in URLs, Install Referrer or client-visible storage.

## Non-regression rules

Do not change:

- approved Ivory Floral Gold artwork, coordinates, 9:19.5 geometry or 1.8-second opening;
- physical-vs-personal invitation identity separation;
- Android mandatory app gating;
- RSVP authorization and stale-guest protection;
- one-time secure install handoff;
- Contributions availability;
- Couple Site ↔ My Wedding round trip;
- token/PII security;
- existing Admin/Planner/Couple/Vendor cold-launch workspace authority.
